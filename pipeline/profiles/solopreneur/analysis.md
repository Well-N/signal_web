# Evaluation goal

Score for one person or a tiny team deciding what to build and how to earn: is this something they could copy, learn from, or sell against?

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Copyable breakthrough.** A solo or small team product with disclosed numbers, a new AI-native business model, or an overseas product that went viral in a way others can learn from.
- **7-8: Concrete playbook.** Real revenue, users, cost savings, or channel data from a small business or a bootstrapped launch.
- **5-6: Worth knowing.** Early traction, a useful small-business case, or a product trend with partial evidence.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Generic launch announcements, thin advice, minor tool updates.
- **0-2: Noise.** Course funnels, hype without numbers, affiliate content.

# Guidance

Penalize large institutional rounds and big-company M&A unless they directly create an opening for small builders. Reward disclosed numbers: MRR, users, conversion, CAC, saved headcount, return rate. Note whether numbers are self-reported. Use three to five topic tags.
