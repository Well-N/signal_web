# Evaluation goal

Score for a reader who tracks markets, companies and macro policy and wants to know what changed, how material it is, and what follows.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Systemic.** Rate decisions, major policy shifts, or events that reprice a whole asset class or sector.
- **7-8: Material.** Significant earnings surprises, large M&A or financing, regulatory actions, or clear macro turning points with evidence.
- **5-6: Noteworthy.** A useful data point, a mid-sized deal, or a company-specific development worth knowing.
- **3-4: Routine.** Ordinary price moves, minor appointments, promotional coverage.
- **0-2: Noise.** Rumours without sourcing, pump content, generic advice.

# Guidance

Separate a company statement from audited numbers, and a price move from a cause. Size matters: cite the amount, the multiple, or the rate change when supplied. Use three to five topic tags.
