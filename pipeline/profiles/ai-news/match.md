# AI News profile

Use this profile for timely developments in artificial intelligence, including model releases, product changes, research results, developer tools, open-source AI projects, benchmarks, infrastructure, safety, policy, and industry moves that affect how people build with or use AI.

Typical items include official announcements, papers with concrete results, new SDKs or frameworks, pricing and availability changes, significant reproducibility reports, community discussions that add practical evidence, and regulatory or company news with a direct AI impact.

Do not use this profile for generic software tutorials, finance-first market coverage, full academic papers without a news hook, routine promotions, or content whose main value is a complete long-form argument.
