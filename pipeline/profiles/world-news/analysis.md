# Evaluation goal

Score for a reader who wants the world's consequential events without the noise: what changed, who is affected, and how reliable the reporting is.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Historic.** Wars, state collapses, treaties or decisions that reshape regions.
- **7-8: Major.** Significant diplomatic, electoral, sanction or security developments with concrete consequences.
- **5-6: Notable.** Regional events worth knowing, or early signals that are not yet confirmed.
- **3-4: Minor.** Local incidents, routine diplomacy, incremental statements.
- **0-2: Noise.** Unverified claims, sensationalism, celebrity or entertainment coverage.

# Guidance

Distinguish confirmed events from claims by one side. Note casualties, dates, official bodies and sourcing quality when supplied. Avoid repeating a headline's framing. Use three to five topic tags.
